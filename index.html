How the day works

  1. Each teacher meets parents in their own classroom. On the day, our staff will guide you to where you need to go.
  2. You book a 10-minute time with your child’s homeroom teacher and meet them first.
  3. After that, you visit the other teachers you would like to see, as they are available. Tick them below and tell us a preferred time if you have one.
  4. Every interview is strictly 10 minutes. Please arrive a few minutes early. If another parent is inside, kindly wait outside the classroom until your time.
  • There will be no classes on the interview day.
  • No interviews during Jumma prayer 1:00 PM – 1:30 PM.
  • Children who come must stay under their parent’s supervision. Students are not allowed to play in the hallway or in the gym.
